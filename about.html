About Daily Banana

Ridiculous measurements for everyday items.

Daily Banana is a daily game about size and spatial thinking. Every question lines up one everyday thing against another: how many hot dogs long is a school bus, how many soda cans tall is a refrigerator, how many paper clips long is a green bean. There is no trick and nothing to look up. It's about picturing things you already know and making a good estimate.

How it works

Each daily has 6 rounds, 25 seconds each. Your points are your accuracy as a percent, so a close guess scores well even when it isn't exact. Every day mixes small counts and huge ones, practical comparisons and silly ones. A new daily arrives at midnight in your time zone, and past dailies, with their answers and how players did, are posted in the archive.

About the sizes

All sizes are approximations: typical averages from publicly available data, rounded for play. Real bananas, buses and teaspoons vary. The game asks you to picture an average one.

Who makes it

Daily Banana is made by Smidgen Games LLC, an independent game studio that publishes tabletop games. Questions or feedback: dailybanana@playsmidgen.com.
