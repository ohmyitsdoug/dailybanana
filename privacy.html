Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026. Daily Banana (dailybanana.io) is a free daily guessing game run by Smidgen Games LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect when you play, why, and what choices you have.

What we collect

Data When Why
Anonymous device ID (a random code stored in your browser) Every visit Count visitors and players without knowing who you are
Visit date Every visit Daily and unique visitor counts
Approximate country (worked out by our hosting provider from your connection; we never save your IP address), your device's time zone and language setting, and whether it's a phone, tablet or computer Every visit See roughly where players are and which devices they use
How long you actively use the game each day, how many times you open it, and the hour (your local time) you finish the daily While you play Understand when and how people play
Daily game plays: your guesses, score and puzzle number When you finish a daily Scores, stats and daily results
Account details from Google: account ID, email address and first name Only if you sign in with Google Create your account and recognize you when you come back
Account details from Discord: user ID, username and display name Only if you sign in with Discord Create your account and recognize you when you come back
Your saved games: daily results, quick play and challenge history, badges and the name you choose Only if you sign in Keep your scores, streak and badges on every device
Challenge entries: the name you enter, your score and emoji results When you make or play a challenge Show the challenge scoreboard

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, payment details, passwords or anything from your device beyond what is listed here. We never see your Google or Discord password. We do not run ads, and we do not sell or rent your information.

Stored on your device

The game saves your settings (like light or dark theme), results, streak, badges, quick play history and, if you sign in, a sign-in token in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser data removes it. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Who helps us run Daily Banana

We share data only with services that help us operate the game, and only what they need:

  • Netlify hosts the website, runs our server functions and stores game and account data.
  • Google provides "Sign in with Google" if you choose to use it, and serves the game's fonts (Google Fonts), which means Google receives your browser's IP address when the page loads.
  • Discord provides "Sign in with Discord" if you choose to use it.

Google and Discord handle your sign-in under their own privacy policies. We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect the game and its players from fraud or abuse.

Public and shared information

Your account name is never shown publicly unless you use it on a challenge. When you make or play a challenge, the name you enter, your score and your emoji results are visible to anyone who has that challenge's link. If you win a weekend challenge, the name you played under and your score may appear in our weekend results posts, including on Discord and our social media. Weekend challenges also show a top-countries list: the country each game came from (worked out by our hosting provider, never from your IP address) is counted anonymously, and only totals are shown, the number of players and average score for countries with at least 10 players. It is never linked to your name, and we don't use it for anything other than showing these numbers. Share text you copy from the results screen contains only your score and emoji grid. Your email address is never shown to other players.

How long we keep it

We keep account details and saved games until you delete your account. Visit and daily play logs are tied to a random device ID and kept while Daily Banana is running so stats stay accurate. Challenges stop taking new players after 24 hours; their scoreboards may be kept so players can look back at them.

Your choices

  • You can play without an account. Everything then stays in your browser on that device.
  • Signed in, you can rename yourself, sign out or delete your account from the game's Menu. Deleting your account removes your account details and saved games from our servers right away.
  • Email us at contact@dailybanana.io to ask for a copy of your data, to remove your name from a challenge scoreboard, or to delete what has been logged from your device. We'll respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU/UK), you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your data. Contact us to use them.

Children

Daily Banana is not directed to children under 13, and accounts are not meant for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS and stored with providers that use industry-standard security. Sign-ins are checked on our server with Google or Discord before an account is opened. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above. For significant changes we'll show a notice in the game.
